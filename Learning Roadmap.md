### SEMESTER 4 — INTERMEDIATE-ADVANCED
* **University Subjects:** Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence Basics, Probability & Statistics, Web Technologies *(align with university syllabus)*

* **University Subjects:** Machine Learning, Data Mining / Data Warehousing, Cloud Computing, Open Electives *(align with university syllabus)*
